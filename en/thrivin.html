Thrivin

High-volume vertical video (9:16) production for Thrivin's Paid Social campaigns — an activewear brand. A performance-driven creative system built on hooks, fast iteration and continuous testing.

The production engine is an AI-assisted pipeline: video generation and animation with models like Kling, Veo or Seedance, product imagery with Nano Banana Pro (ghost mannequin, laydowns, lifestyle and chroma-key), and editing plus template systems in Final Cut Pro and Motion.

This workflow runs at scale: multiple briefs in parallel with several hook variants per concept, maximising creative testing without compromising a premium finish.

Content is split into two formats: UGC, with an organic, platform-native tone, and Demo, focused on product, benefit and technical detail.
